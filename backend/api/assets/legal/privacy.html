Privacy Policy

Talk to My Trip · Effective date: July 11, 2026

What the app does

Talk to My Trip is a voice travel assistant. You speak with an assistant that can plan a trip, book its parts — flight, hotel, ride, dining, and activities — and repair the itinerary when something goes wrong, while a live timeline of your trip is shown on screen.

Microphone and voice audio

When you start a conversation, the app uses your microphone. Your voice audio is streamed to Vocal Bridge, the speech service that converts what you say into text and speaks the assistant's replies back to you. Audio is used to hold the conversation — the app does not use it for anything else.

What we store

To operate the service, we store conversation transcripts (the text of what you and the assistant said) and your trip data (the trips and itinerary items you plan and book) in our database, hosted on Google Cloud (BigQuery).

What we don't do

  • No accounts — the app never asks you to sign up or log in.
  • No advertising.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No sale of your data, ever.

Data deletion

To ask for your conversation transcripts or trip data to be deleted, email us at joshjanzen@gmail.com and we will remove it.
