Privacy Policy

Talk to My Trip · Effective date: July 11, 2026

What the app does

Talk to My Trip is a voice travel assistant. You speak with an assistant that can plan a trip, book its parts — flight, hotel, ride, dining, and activities — and repair the itinerary when something goes wrong, while a live timeline of your trip is shown on screen.

Consent

Before your first conversation, the app shows a consent screen explaining what is shared and with whom; the microphone is not activated until you agree. You can decline ("Not now") and still use the rest of the app — the live trip timeline works without voice.

Microphone and voice audio

When you start a conversation, the app uses your microphone. Your voice audio is streamed to Vocal Bridge, the speech service that converts what you say into text and speaks the assistant's replies back to you. Audio is used to hold the conversation — the app does not use it for anything else.

AI processing and third parties

Three service providers process data on our behalf, each only for the purpose named here:

  • Vocal Bridge — receives your voice audio to run the spoken conversation (speech-to-text and text-to-speech).
  • OpenAI — receives the text of the conversation to generate the assistant's replies and carry out trip actions. This is AI processing: what you say to the assistant is analyzed by an AI model.
  • Google Cloud — hosts our backend and stores transcripts and trip data (BigQuery).

Each provider processes this data as a service provider under its own privacy and data-protection terms, providing protection equivalent to this policy; none of them is permitted to use your data for advertising or to sell it.

What we store

To operate the service, we store conversation transcripts (the text of what you and the assistant said) and your trip data (the trips and itinerary items you plan and book) in our database, hosted on Google Cloud (BigQuery).

Data retention

Talk to My Trip is a voice travel assistant. Conversation transcripts, voice recordings, and trip data are retained only while the service operates and are deleted no later than 90 days after collection — or sooner, on request.

What we don't do

  • No accounts — the app never asks you to sign up or log in.
  • No advertising.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No sale of your data, ever.

Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw voice-data consent at any time in the app: open the About screen (the info button) and choose Withdraw voice consent. The current conversation ends, and the microphone is not used again unless you consent again. You can also revoke microphone access in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. Withdrawal stops all future voice processing; data already collected is covered by the deletion process below.

Data deletion

To ask for your conversation transcripts or trip data to be deleted, email us at joshjanzen@gmail.com and we will remove it within 30 days.
